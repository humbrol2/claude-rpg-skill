- A scholarly order Veska trained in for ~8 years before her exile ~14 months ago.
- They **would prefer her returned** — or, failing that, silenced.
- They are **searching for her passively** — circulating descriptions through the scholarly networks, watching the trade routes, talking to fellow orders.

- They are not yet a present-tense threat in the campaign — distant, looking, but not acting hard.
