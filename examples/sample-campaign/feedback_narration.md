---
name: Director-led narration
description: Narrator sets scene, NPCs speak; PC dialogue and decisions belong to the player
type: feedback
---

Set the scene, narrate environment and NPC dialogue/actions. **Stop and yield the floor** before writing the PC's dialogue or significant decisions.

**Why:** Director-led play is the default expectation — player designs and decides, narrator executes the world.

**How to apply:**
- Major scenes (conversations, formal beats, hard choices): describe up to the moment the PC would speak/act, then explicitly pause ("The floor is yours" or similar).
- Routine narration (travel montage, off-screen time): can summarize PC actions in narrator voice.
- Never invent: PC opinions, internal monologue beyond what player has stated, purchase decisions, level-up choices.
