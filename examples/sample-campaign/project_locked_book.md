# The locked book

## Established

- A bound volume, ~400 pages, leather over wood boards.
- **Sealed** with a mechanical clasp Veska cannot bypass on her own.
- She has carried it since her exile (~14 months).
- She believes the book is meant to reach a specific scholar somewhere in the eastern reaches but does not currently know who.
- The book bears no exterior markings that identify the recipient.

## Open / TBD (do not invent)

- Contents
- Identity of intended recipient
- What unlocks the clasp (key? blood? specific ritual? voice?)
- Whether the Order knows Veska has it
- Whether the book is dangerous, valuable, both, or neither

## Notes for narrator

- Veska handles the book carefully. She does not show it to strangers casually.
- If asked about it, she dissembles or refuses — not yet a trust-test the player has been offered.
