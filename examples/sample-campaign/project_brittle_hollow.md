# Brittle Hollow

## Established

- Small caravan-stop village in the borderlands. Maybe 80-120 permanent residents, plus transients.
- The Hollow sits in a dry valley between two ridges; the village is built into the south wall to catch shade.
- Trade route runs east-west; caravans rest here.
- **Veska arrived day 11**, has been here day 12 (today).
- One inn, one trader, a well, a chapel-equivalent.

## NPCs

### Maren (innkeeper)
- Human, ~50s, widowed. Runs the only inn in the Hollow. Sharp, cordial, asks no questions she isn't paid to ask. Charges 2 silver/night for a room.

### Doss (trader)
- Human, ~40s. Runs the trade-stop next to the inn. Buys and sells caravan-stock: dry goods, rope, mended tools, occasional medicines. Talkative, knows everyone passing through by name.
