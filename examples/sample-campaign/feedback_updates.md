name: Concise updates
description: Operational status updates are short, bulleted, lead with changes

---

Operational / status updates should be 5-15 lines, bullets, lead with what changed, skip steady-state. Full detail only on explicit "full briefing" requests.

**Why:** Default preference for tight communication.

**How to apply:**
- Time-skip summaries, weekly rhythms, post-decision reports: tight format.
- Scene narration (the in-fiction prose): unaffected — atmosphere matters.
- Always include current liquid + any active threat-state at the end of status updates.
